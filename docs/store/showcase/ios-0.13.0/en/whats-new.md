## Paste-ready (≈1 600 chars, limit 4 000)

Meet Dinarly 0.13.0 — a big update since 0.8.0. Same app, new name, and a lot of new power.

• New name: Dinarly (formerly Sovereign Ledger).
• Home Screen widgets: follow a market at a glance, add a mini converter or a currency grid — widgets open the right page, even in dark and tinted modes.
• Siri & Shortcuts: convert any amount, check an exchange rate, review history, or manage your rate alerts hands-free.
• Made for iPad: adaptive layout with a sidebar, two-column Home, native slide transitions, and a two-pane welcome flow.
• New languages: German, Arabic (full right-to-left), Italian, Spanish, Portuguese — choose in settings; currency names follow your language too.
• Faster history: rate charts open instantly from a local cache and reach further back in time.
• Smarter converter: pick the From currency independently of your markets, with a quick swap button.
• Easier market management: swipe to remove a market, or add one right from your account.
• Personalize it: add your name during onboarding and see it on your Home.

Enjoying Dinarly? Rate us on the App Store — it helps a lot.
